*   **Processing:** Keep untouched original. No normalization baked into master. No limiter. No unnecessary compression. Preserve clean headroom and long handles before/after.
*   **File Naming:** `[ID]_[DESCRIPTION]_[GEN/REC]_v[XX]_[STATUS].wav` (e.g., `SFX-03_TRAFFIC_CITYBED_GEN_v01_RAW.wav`).
